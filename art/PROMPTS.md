# Prompts for runner's painted art (P562)

Paste one prompt into an image tool, then save the result in `art/source/` under the name given. The converter scales each picture down, removes the flat background colour and writes the board format. So make the pictures large (1024 px or more), and keep the background exactly the flat colour named.

Every prompt ends with the same style sentence, so the pictures match.

## Why the flat colours differ

Each sprite or layer sits on a flat colour that the picture itself does not use: green behind magenta things, magenta behind green things, navy behind white clouds. The converter removes that colour. Soft watercolour edges are blended onto the sky colour of their row, which is exact, because the sky changes only from top to bottom.
