Wire the rotary encoder, step by step

Four wires join the CJMCU-111 encoder to your breadboard. Every wire you already have stays where it is. Find each pad by the name printed on the board. Use the buttons or the arrow keys.

1

Where each fact comes from:

  • The four wires: spark's breadboard sheet for P758 (build/breadboard.md, steps 5, 6, 16, 19 and 20), built from requirements.json. spark chose SDA and SCL, because every other free pin is wired or is the board's own button.
  • The pad order of both rows: spark's board record. Both rows start flush at the end away from USB-C.
  • The encoder's pads: the part record cjmcu-111-rotary-encoder (GA at the end away from the tan capacitor). VCC from 3.3 V only, never 5 V.
  • No pull-up resistors are confirmed on the encoder, so the firmware turns on the chip's own pull-ups. The meter reading in step 7 tells whether the encoder has its own.
