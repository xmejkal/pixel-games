The new job on the same hardware

- USER-12 · What a 96 px figure leaves of 320 by 240 (calc). Today a Rescue frame is 26 x 30 px (1,560 bytes, offsets 154064 to 155624 in the pack) at FIG_H 26. A 96 px figure is about 3.7 times as tall. At the same aspect the frame is about 83 x 96 px, so 3 whole figures fit side by side (3.85), with no room to pass each other. Of 240 px of height it is 40 %, leaving 144 px above (the HUD bar and the sky). Rescue's street is the hips' travel from LEFT_X 74 to RIGHT_X 304, 230 px, which is 8.8 of today's 26 px figures and 2.4 of a 96 px figure. Today's up to 5 people plus the fireman (CROWD up to 5) cannot stand on a 230 px street at 96 px. · E for the constants: art/sets/rescue.py:172, games/art_index.py:90-104, games/rescue.py:35-36, 61, 100 (CROWD, STREET_LEFT/RIGHT); H (calc) for the results · supports: a scene of a 96 px figure holds two or three figures, not a crowd. Does not support that two fit while one passes a third.
- USER-13 · How far one walks before the scene must scroll (calc). Rescue's fireman runs 56 px/s at 24.07 px a body height (2.3 body heights/s, a mocap speed). At 96 px, with the motion capture's own speed, that is about 220 to 230 px/s: he crosses the 230 px street, or most of a 320 px screen, in about 1.1 to 1.4 s. The granny's 18 px/s becomes about 70 to 85 px/s. Kept at today's px/s the feet would slide, because the pose follows the distance run (a 41 px stride today, about 150 px at 96 px). So a screen holds one second of running, and a mission that needs walking from one end to the other needs either a scroll (a wider scene) or slower-than-capture walking. The box has scrolled bands before: the runner's art holds 640 px wide layers (clouds 640 x 94, far 640 x 100, ground 640 x 50), and the panel's scroll along game x moves left and right bands. · E · games/rescue.py:36-37, 39-43, 99-100; games/art_index.py:11-17; bench.md:65 · supports: the scene is wider than the screen if a mission has a distance. Not measured: the frame time of a scrolled 96 px figure.
- USER-14 · What a 96 px set costs in art memory (calc).
