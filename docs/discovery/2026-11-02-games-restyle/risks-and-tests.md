## Budgets <!-- REQUIRED:electronics-firmware:budgets -->
<!-- Energy per mode · peaks per rail against the source · dissipation per part, each number with its provenance and
the rung that would settle it (electronics-firmware:R<n>). "n/a — no battery, motor, radio or regulator" is allowed. -->
Energy, rails and dissipation: n/a — no battery, motor, radio or regulator changes; the board is USB-powered in play and no hardware changes. The budgets this item spends are frame time and memory, measured by the tech lead (council/tech-lead.md, the budget table):
- frame pace 40 ms (stated, games/app.py:26); step plus paint 39 to 43 ms with no play (measured, origin/p556-double-buffer bench.md); fps 20 to 24 (measured); worst gap 63 to 69 ms (measured); headroom none (calculated);
- full-frame send 30.72 ms calculated at the real 40 MHz, 31.0 to 33.4 ms measured (bench.md:33, :84; origin bench.md:186); copy into the outgoing buffer 3.2 to 3.4 ms (measured);
- blit: opaque 34.5 ns/px, keyed 51.6 ns/px upper bound, translucent 126 ns/px (measured, internal destination, bench.md:167, :169; P567 measured 323 to 338 ns/px for 64x64 at alpha 128 in both frames, run46-p567-*.log, so the 126 figure is from another shape and is not used for budgets); PSRAM destination +8.4 to +9.7 ms per frame on the same art (measured, P556 slice 0), per primitive measured by P567 #772 (bench.md P567 section on branch p567-probe, run46-p567-*.log): full-frame opaque blit 6.83 ms in PSRAM against 2.58 ms internal, keyed 6.86 against 3.56 ms; a keyed 16x24 sprite 23.8 µs warm, 67.8 µs right after a full-frame blit; 64x64 at alpha 128 337.5 ns/px; pictures.draw of a 16x24 sprite 69.4 µs; Python physics 57 µs per particle and 114 µs per spring body per update (measured);
- memory: art 1,506,384 B for three games (measured, bench.md:176); ten games about 5.02 MB, so about 3.5 MB more than today (calculated); PSRAM free 8,319,680 B before the pool (bench.md:172) and 6,813,040 B with it (origin bench.md:186; measured, IDF, heap split measured by P567: after deinit PSRAM free 6,747,500 B with a largest block of 6,553,600 B, gc free 6,603,376 B, gc alloc 78,304 B, and a 4,000,000 B bytearray allocates (run46-p567-double.log); that the GC heap and the pool draw on the same PSRAM is H, from MicroPython's split heap, not shown by a log line; the difference of 1,506,640 B is the pool plus 256 B, H arithmetic); internal DMA largest block 31,744 B after "double" (measured, origin bench.md:186; one of the 32 KB heaps, so no total is derived from it); file system 12,800,000 B free of 14,680,064 B, art on the board 1,507,152 B (measured, P567, run46-p567-double.log); firmware image 1,867,376 B with 229,776 B free in the app partition (measured, bench.md:6).
