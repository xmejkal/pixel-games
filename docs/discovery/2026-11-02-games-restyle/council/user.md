## Findings

Frame pace is 40 ms, 25 fps at best (games/app.py:35). Measured 20 to 24 fps (README.md:32; bench.md:116). Text is an 8x8 font, 8 px high (games/gfx.py:106-115).

The title is growing rectangles plus the game name in 3x font on a black band, a blinking hint, and "best N". Game over is a black 200x100 box with a grey inner box, "game over" in red, "score N best N", blinking "tap to retry". Only `accent` and `name` differ.
