<!-- Energy per mode · peaks per rail against the source · dissipation per part, each number with its provenance and

Energy, rails and dissipation: n/a — no battery, motor, radio or regulator changes now. The board is USB-powered in play. A radio comes later (D2), and its draw and peaks are read at their source when that card starts. The budgets this item spends are frame time and memory:
- frame: pace 40 ms, so a 25 fps cap (stated, games/app.py:26, calculated). Stack pure-play frame 29.2 ms = step 1.58 + paint 22.62 + send 3.21 + gc 1.78 ms, so 10.8 ms headroom (measured, run52-p582-bot.log, 33 intervals, parsed by the tech lead, TL-9). Slowest-frame step 14 to 16 ms in four of the nine intervals with over or title frames, which also carry about 9 ms of gc (measured, run 52, a bot, TL-11, re-parsed by the refuter). The log is git-ignored and kept on this Mac only. Flappy and runner paint 39 to 43 ms, no headroom (measured, bench.md P556, TL-10). Settles: R2 for logic, R6 for time.
- per object: Python particle 56.8 µs, Python spring body 114.0 µs (measured, run46, bytecode). Picture 16 x 24 69.43 µs. Empty Python-to-C call 5.5 µs (measured, run26). Today's `fall_bodies` (`@native`) per body on the board: — (the P589 baseline). C step per body: — (R6 probe, P589).
- allocation: `Bodies.step` 544 B per call, `Spring.step` 112 B (measured, run49), 13,056 B/s at 24 fps (calculated). gc 1.78 ms per frame on average (measured, run 52).
- memory: largest internal DMA block 31,744 B while "double" runs (measured, bench.md:186). A 64-body world of 12 float32 fields is 3,072 B, and a 256-entry event ring of 12 B is 3,072 B (calculated, TL-16). PSRAM free 6,747,500 and 6,813,040 B in two runs, with 1,507,152 B of art (measured, bench.md P556 section, run41 and one more run, as the refuter read them). Free internal non-DMA RAM in play: — (R5, one `esp32.idf_heap_info(esp32.HEAP_DATA)` line).
